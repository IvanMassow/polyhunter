Questions, answered straight

The things you’d ask us at a conference bar.

Is this compliance advice?

No — and the distinction matters. Hector assembles, organises and drafts from your evidence and your decisions. It doesn’t judge whether a product is suitable or a practice compliant; that stays with you and, if you have one, your compliance consultant. Think of Hector as the world’s most diligent compliance administrator, not a compliance officer.

What happens when the FCA changes the rules?

That’s rather the point. Hector reads FCA publications as they land, works out what applies to a firm of your size and permissions, updates the relevant parts of your file, and tells you in plain English what changed and what (if anything) you need to do. Static policy libraries go stale; Hector rewrites itself.

I already pay a compliance consultancy. Why would I need this?

Keep them — for judgement. Most retainers still leave the evidence-gathering, MI and report-writing to you. Firms use Hector to do that administration continuously, then spend fewer consultancy hours on higher-value review. Some replace the retainer entirely; that’s your call, not our pitch.

Where does my data live, and who can see it?

UK-hosted, encrypted at rest and in transit, with a full audit trail of every access. Your client data is yours: we don’t train models on it, we don’t share it, and you can export or delete everything at any time. Full security documentation will be published before launch — we hold ourselves to the standard we help you evidence.

How long does setup take?

The guided intake takes about 40 minutes, and most firms have a working evidence vault the same day. No integration project, no consultant on site — if you can upload a PDF and answer plain-English questions about your firm, you can set up Hector.

What does the free readiness score involve?

Fifteen questions, five minutes, no credit card. You get a scored assessment of how your firm stands against the Consumer Duty evidence expectations the FCA set out in its board-report review — and which gaps to close first, whether or not you ever pay us a penny.
