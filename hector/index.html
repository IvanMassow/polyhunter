The problem

The Duty wasn’t written with firms like yours in mind. The work still landed on your desk.

If you run a small advice firm or brokerage, you already know the shape of it. You are the SMF holder, the data analyst, the MI department and the report author — after client hours.

Consumer Duty asks for ongoing outcomes monitoring, an annual board report and an annual confirmation of compliance. SM&CR asks for responsibility maps, fitness-and-propriety records and certification evidence. None of it is optional, and none of it cares that your “compliance function” is you, on a Sunday.

“Smaller firms have different challenges… limited management information, no dedicated compliance or audit function.”

— FCA, Consumer Duty board reports review

The regulator’s own answer was to suggest small firms find a “critical friend.” Hector is that friend — one who never sleeps, reads every FCA publication the day it lands, and keeps your evidence file ready for the day the email arrives.

Questions, answered straight

The things you’d ask us at a conference bar.

Is this compliance advice?

No — and we’re glad you asked, because the distinction matters. Hector assembles, organises and drafts from your evidence and your decisions. It doesn’t tell you whether a product is suitable or a practice is compliant; that judgement stays with you and, if you have one, your compliance consultant. Think of Hector as the world’s most diligent compliance administrator, not a compliance officer.

What happens when the FCA changes the rules?

That’s rather the point. Hector reads FCA publications as they land, works out what applies to a firm of your size and permissions, updates the relevant parts of your file, and tells you in plain English what changed and what (if anything) you need to do. Static policy libraries go stale; Hector rewrites itself.

I already pay a compliance consultancy. Why would I need this?

Keep them — for judgement. Most retainers still leave the evidence-gathering, MI and report-writing to you. Firms use Hector to do that administration continuously, then spend fewer consultancy hours on higher-value review. Some firms replace the retainer entirely; that’s your call, not our pitch.

Where does my data live, and who can see it?

UK-hosted, encrypted at rest and in transit, with a full audit trail of every access. Your client data is yours: we don’t train models on it, we don’t share it, and you can export or delete everything at any time. We’ll publish our full security documentation before launch — and we’ll hold ourselves to the standard we help you evidence.

How long does it take to get set up?

The guided intake takes about 40 minutes, and most firms have a working evidence vault the same day. There’s no integration project and no consultant on site — if you can upload a PDF and answer plain-English questions about your firm, you can set up Hector.

What does the free readiness score involve?

Fifteen questions, five minutes, no credit card. You’ll get a scored assessment of how your firm currently stands against the Consumer Duty evidence expectations the FCA set out in its board-report review — and exactly which gaps to close first, whether or not you ever pay us a penny.
