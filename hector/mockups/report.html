4. Price and value outcome

The firm completed its annual fee schedule review on 28 May 2026 E-147, benchmarking ongoing advice charges against published peer data for firms of comparable size and permissions. Charges for the core advice proposition remain within the second quartile of the comparator set E-147, and no client segment was identified as receiving materially lower value relative to the fees paid.

Ongoing service delivery was evidenced through completion of 96% of scheduled annual reviews in the period E-112, against 91% in the prior year. The four reviews not completed related to clients who deferred at their own request; each deferral is documented with a follow-up date E-113.

One instance was identified in which a legacy client remained on a fee basis withdrawn in 2023 E-129. The client was migrated to current terms with a refund of the difference, and a vault-wide check confirmed no further clients on withdrawn terms E-131.

Hector flagged one gap: no documented assessment of value for clients holding only protection products. Suggested action created — a short protection-book value review (est. 2 hours). This section will update when filed.

On the basis of the evidence above, the board is invited to conclude that the firm’s services continue to provide fair value, subject to completion of the protection-book review noted.
